Our process

Four steps, repeated as needed. You always know what we are doing, what we need from you, and what you will receive.

01

Audit

We review your website, search visibility, analytics, and conversion paths to understand where you stand today. This becomes the baseline we measure progress against.

You provide

  • Your website address and main business goals
  • Read-only access to analytics and Search Console, if available
  • A short call or questionnaire about your customers

You receive

  • A written audit report in plain language
  • A prioritised list of issues and opportunities
  • A walkthrough call to discuss the findings

Typical duration: [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. 1 to 2 weeks]

02

Strategy

We agree what success looks like and turn the audit findings into a plan. The plan is short, specific, and ordered by expected effort and impact.

You provide

  • Feedback on the audit findings
  • Budget range, timeline, and any constraints
  • Who on your side will approve and implement changes

You receive

  • A written plan with clear priorities
  • Agreed goals and how each will be measured
  • A timeline and an estimate for the work

Typical duration: [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. 1 week]

03

Implement

We make the agreed changes, or give your team or developer clear specifications and review their work. You see progress as it happens, not only at the end.

You provide

  • Access to the website and relevant accounts
  • Timely approval of content and changes
  • Product, service, or customer knowledge we need

You receive

  • The agreed changes, completed and checked
  • Short progress updates at an agreed frequency
  • Documentation of what was changed and why

Typical duration: [PLACEHOLDER: depends on scope, e.g. 4 to 12 weeks]

04

Measure and improve

We compare results with the baseline from the audit, explain what changed, and recommend what to do next. Then the cycle continues with the next set of priorities.

You provide

  • Business context we cannot see in the data, such as sales or seasonality
  • Time for a regular review call

You receive

  • A plain-language report on results against goals
  • Recommendations for the next round of work
  • An updated plan, if priorities have changed

Reporting frequency: [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. monthly]
