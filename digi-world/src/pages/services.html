Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

In plain English: GEO means making your content easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity to understand, trust, and mention when they answer questions.

We review how AI tools currently describe your business, then improve your content, structure, and references so you are represented accurately and more often.

What you get: an AI-visibility review, a content and structured-data plan, and periodic checks of how AI tools describe you.

Ask about GEO

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

In plain English: AEO means writing and structuring your content as clear, direct answers so search engines and voice assistants can show it when people ask a question.

We find the questions your customers ask, write short answers to them, and add the page structure and markup that help search engines and voice assistants use those answers.

What you get: a question map for your market, answer-led page and FAQ content, and structured data (schema) added to key pages.

Questions

Questions about our services

What is the difference between SEO, GEO, and AEO?

SEO helps your pages appear in search results. GEO helps AI tools understand and mention your business in the answers they write. AEO structures your content as direct answers that search engines and voice assistants can show. All three rely on clear, accurate, well-structured content.

Which service should I start with?

Most businesses should start with a website audit. It shows what is working, what is not, and which services will make the biggest difference, so you do not pay for work you do not need.

Why does GA4 setup matter?

Without reliable measurement, you cannot tell which marketing is working. A correct GA4 setup records the actions that matter, such as enquiries and purchases, so decisions are based on evidence.

Can you work with my existing web developer or agency?

Yes. We can provide clear specifications for your developer, review their changes, or make the changes ourselves, depending on what suits your team.

Do you guarantee rankings or a specific return on investment?

No. Search engines and AI tools control what they show, and results depend on many factors. We agree clear goals at the start, report honestly on progress, and adjust the plan based on the data.
