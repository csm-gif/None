GA4 analytics and measurement

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is Google's free tool for measuring what visitors do on your website. We set it up or repair it so it records the actions that matter to you, such as enquiries, calls, and purchases.

Good fit if
You don't trust your numbers, or you don't know where enquiries come from.
What you get
A documented GA4 setup with key events, consent-aware tracking, and a simple dashboard, plus a handover call.
Read: what to check in your GA4 setup

SEO

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the work of helping search engines like Google find, understand, and show your pages. We fix technical problems, improve key pages, and make your site structure clear.

Good fit if
You aren't showing up for searches your customers make.
What you get
A prioritised SEO plan, technical and on-page fixes, and regular visibility reporting.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

In plain English: GEO means making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity to understand, trust, and mention in their answers.

We check how AI tools describe your business today. Then we improve your content and your presence on other trusted sites, so the description is accurate and more complete. No one controls what AI tools say; we improve the information they draw on.

Good fit if
Customers are starting to ask AI tools for recommendations in your field.
What you get
An AI-visibility review, a content plan, and regular checks of how AI tools describe you.
Read: SEO vs GEO vs AEO explained

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

In plain English: AEO means writing your content as clear, direct answers to real questions, so search engines and voice assistants can show those answers.

We find the questions your customers ask, write short answers to them, and structure your pages so those answers are easy to find and quote.

Good fit if
Your customers ask a lot of similar questions before they buy.
What you get
A list of your customers' key questions, answer-led page content, and structured data on key pages.

Conversion rate optimization

Conversion rate optimization (CRO) means helping more of your current visitors take the next step. We find where people hesitate or leave, then test specific changes.

Good fit if
You get visitors but few enquiries or sales.
What you get
A review of key pages and forms, a ranked list of changes to test, and test results.
Read: why your traffic isn't converting

It depends on the work. Fixes to tracking and conversion can show an effect within weeks. SEO and AI-answer visibility usually take several months, because search engines and AI tools need time to notice changes. We agree a realistic timeline at the start and measure against it.

Your website address, your main business goal, and read-only access to Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console if you have them. We also ask you to answer a short questionnaire about your customers.
