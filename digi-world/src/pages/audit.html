What we review

Six areas, one ranked list

  • GA4 setup and data quality

    Whether your analytics records the actions that matter (enquiries, calls, sales) and whether you can trust the numbers.

  • Technical SEO

    Whether search engines can find, crawl, and index your important pages. We also look at speed, mobile use, and errors.

  • On-page SEO

    Whether each key page clearly matches what people search for, with good titles, headings, and internal links.

  • Content and AI-answer readiness

    Whether your pages answer real customer questions clearly enough to be shown by search engines, voice assistants, and AI tools (AEO and GEO).

  • Conversion path

    How easy it is for a visitor to understand your offer, trust you, and contact you or buy, including forms and mobile use.

  • Channel tracking

    Whether you can tell which channels (search, social, email, ads) bring visitors who turn into customers.
