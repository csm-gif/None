Questions

Common questions

Short, direct answers. If yours is not here, ask us.

What does Digi World do?

Digi World is a digital growth consultancy. We help small and mid-sized businesses get more qualified website visitors and enquiries through SEO, GA4 analytics, AI-search visibility, conversion work, and clear strategy.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the work of making your content easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity to understand, trust, and mention when they answer questions.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the work of writing and structuring your content as clear, direct answers so search engines and voice assistants can show it when people ask a question.

What is included in a website audit?

A website audit covers technical health, search visibility, AI-answer readiness, analytics setup, and conversion paths. You receive a written report with plain-language findings and a prioritised list of fixes.

Do you guarantee search rankings or results?

No. Search engines and AI tools decide what they show, so no one can honestly guarantee rankings. We commit to clear work, honest reporting, and measuring the results so you can see what changed.
