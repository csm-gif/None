A practical, evidence-led approach to digital growth

Digi World helps small and mid-sized businesses grow online by doing the basics well, measuring honestly, and improving step by step.

Why we exist

Clear advice for businesses without a large marketing team

Many businesses spend money on digital marketing without knowing what works. Reports are full of numbers but short on meaning. Advice changes with each new trend.

We take a different approach. We start by measuring what is happening today. We explain what we find in plain language. Then we agree a short list of changes that are most likely to make a difference, and we check whether they did.

We work with owners, founders, and marketing leads at small and mid-sized businesses, both in local markets and internationally.

How we work

Our principles

These guide every recommendation we make and every report we send.

  1. Evidence before opinion

    We base recommendations on your data and your market, not on trends or habits. When the evidence is unclear, we say so.

  2. Plain, honest reporting

    Reports explain what changed, why it matters, and what we will do next. We report results that disappoint as clearly as results that please.

  3. Foundations first

    Reliable measurement, a fast and accessible website, and clear content come before campaigns. They make every later step more effective.

  4. Small steps, measured

    We prefer a few focused changes that we can measure over large projects that are hard to evaluate.

  5. No promises we cannot keep

    No one controls search rankings or AI answers. We do not guarantee them. We commit to clear goals, good work, and honest measurement.

  6. You own your accounts and data

    Analytics, ad, and search accounts are set up in your name, so you keep full access whether or not we keep working together.
